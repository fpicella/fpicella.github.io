Teaching

Sorbonne Université, Faculté des Sciences et Ingénierie — all my teaching is in the first semester. Course material and assignments are on Moodle Sciences (students only).

  • UM5MEF02 — Pratique de la simulation numérique · M2, 3 ECTS, coordinator. Hands-on CFD with OpenFOAM: finite volumes from 1D advection up to incompressible Navier–Stokes, turbulence and flows around bodies, every step checked against theory or the literature. Moodle
  • UM4MEF11 — Méthodes numériques en mécanique des fluides 1 · M1, 3 ECTS, coordinator. Finite-difference methods for the PDEs of fluid mechanics: accuracy and stability, explicit and implicit time stepping, diffusion and convection schemes, a 2D convection–diffusion project in Python/Jupyter. Moodle
  • UL2ME007 and UL2ME114 · L2 Mécanique — tutorials and lab work. Moodle
